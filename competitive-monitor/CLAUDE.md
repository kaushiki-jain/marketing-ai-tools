# Competitive Positioning Monitor — SAP Concur

## Purpose
Analyse competitor messaging and positioning to identify 
gaps and differentiation opportunities for SAP Concur in 
the enterprise travel and expense management market.

## SAP Concur's Core Position
- Market leader in integrated travel and expense management
- Built for enterprise — scales across global organisations
- Deep ERP integrations especially with SAP ecosystem
- Strong compliance, audit, and policy enforcement features
- Trusted by finance teams and CFOs

## Target Buyers
### Primary: CFO / Finance Director
- Cares about: cost control, compliance, audit trails, ROI
- Pain points: manual reconciliation, expense fraud, 
  lack of visibility into spend
- Language: ROI-focused, risk reduction, financial control

### Secondary: Travel Manager / HR
- Cares about: employee experience, policy compliance, 
  duty of care
- Pain points: booking outside policy, lack of reporting,
  manual processes
- Language: efficiency, employee satisfaction, visibility

### Tertiary: IT / Procurement
- Cares about: security, integrations, implementation
- Pain points: complex integrations, data security, 
  change management
- Language: enterprise-grade, scalable, secure

## Competitors to Monitor
1. Expensify — SMB focused, simple UX, strong brand voice
2. Navan (formerly TripActions) — modern UI, travel-first approach
3. Ramp — finance automation, fast growing, strong positioning
4. Coupa — enterprise spend management, procurement focused
5. Zoho Expense — cost-effective, SMB and mid-market

## What to Analyse
- Core value proposition
- Target audience language
- Key messages and claims
- Tone of voice
- What they emphasise vs avoid
- Pricing messaging
- Any obvious gaps or weaknesses
- How they position against enterprise vs SMB

## Output Rules
- Be analytical not just descriptive
- Always include a "so what" for SAP Concur
- Flag genuine differentiation opportunities
- Keep it actionable — this is for a PMM, not a researcher
- Separate findings by competitor
- End with a strategic summary

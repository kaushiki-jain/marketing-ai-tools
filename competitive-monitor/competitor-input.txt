# Competitor Messaging Research — T&E Management Market

## Expensify
Tagline: "Expense reports that don't suck"
Value proposition: The easiest way to manage expenses.
Expensify automates expense reports, receipt scanning,
and reimbursements for businesses of all sizes.
Key messages:
- Receipt scanning in one click with SmartScan
- Automatic expense report creation
- Fast reimbursements — next day ACH
- Free for individuals, simple pricing for teams
- Loved by employees, not just finance teams
Tone: Casual, irreverent, employee-first
Target audience: SMBs, startups, individual employees
Pricing message: Simple, transparent, affordable
What they emphasise: Speed, simplicity, employee experience
Notable: Very strong brand voice, anti-corporate tone

## Navan (formerly TripActions)
Tagline: "The only integrated travel and expense solution"
Value proposition: Navan combines travel booking,
expense management, and corporate cards in one platform.
Real-time visibility into every dollar spent.
Key messages:
- All-in-one travel and expense platform
- Real-time spend visibility for finance teams
- Modern UI employees actually want to use
- Significant savings on travel spend
- 24/7 travel support included
Tone: Modern, confident, growth-focused
Target audience: Mid-market and enterprise,
  finance and travel managers
Pricing message: ROI and savings focused
What they emphasise: Integration of travel and expense,
  modern experience, real-time data
Notable: Strong on UI/UX messaging,
  positions legacy tools as outdated

## Ramp
Tagline: "The finance automation platform"
Value proposition: Ramp helps businesses spend less
and close their books faster with corporate cards,
expense management, bill payments, and accounting
automation — all in one platform.
Key messages:
- Save an average of 5% on company spending
- Close the books 8x faster
- Finance automation not just expense management
- Real-time spend controls
- Integrates with every major accounting tool
Tone: Data-driven, confident, CFO-focused
Target audience: Finance teams, CFOs, fast-growing companies
Pricing message: Free to use — makes money on interchange
What they emphasise: Savings, speed, automation,
  financial intelligence
Notable: Very strong on proof points and specific claims,
  positions as intelligence platform not just expense tool

## Coupa
Tagline: "The business spend management platform"
Value proposition: Coupa provides a unified platform
for business spend management — procurement, invoicing,
expenses, and payments — giving CFOs total visibility
and control over company spend.
Key messages:
- Total spend visibility across the entire organisation
- AI-powered spend intelligence
- Strong compliance and audit capabilities
- Enterprise-grade security and controls
- Community intelligence from $6 trillion in spend data
Tone: Enterprise, authoritative, data-heavy
Target audience: Large enterprises, CFOs, procurement teams
Pricing message: Enterprise pricing, ROI focused
What they emphasise: Total spend management,
  AI intelligence, compliance
Notable: Closest to SAP Concur in enterprise positioning,
  very strong on data and AI narrative

## Zoho Expense
Tagline: "Smart expense reporting software"
Value proposition: Automate your expense reporting
process end to end. From receipt scanning to
reimbursements, Zoho Expense handles it all at
a price that works for every business.
Key messages:
- Affordable for every business size
- Part of the Zoho ecosystem
- Easy to use, quick to implement
- Multi-currency and multi-entity support
- Strong mobile app
Tone: Friendly, accessible, value-focused
Target audience: SMBs and mid-market,
  cost-conscious buyers
Pricing message: Very affordable, clear tiers
What they emphasise: Value for money, ease of use,
  ecosystem integration
Notable: Competes on price,
  not trying to win on enterprise features

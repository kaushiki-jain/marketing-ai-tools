# GTM Launch Kit Generator

A lightweight, prompt-driven tool that turns a short product brief into a complete go-to-market launch kit — positioning, messaging, persona-specific copy, launch email, sales enablement materials, ad headlines, and a launch timeline.

## What This Tool Does

Given a simple product description (name, what it does, who it's for, key differentiator, and launch date), this tool generates a full GTM launch kit covering positioning, messaging, persona-specific content, launch communications, sales enablement, ad copy, and a launch timeline — all in one pass.

## Why It Matters for Product Marketers

Launch prep typically means producing the same core narrative in a dozen different formats — positioning docs, sales one-pagers, customer emails, ad copy — each tailored to a different audience. This tool front-loads that work:

- **Speed:** Go from a product brief to a full first-draft launch kit in minutes, not days.
- **Consistency:** Every output is grounded in the same positioning and messaging pillars, so sales, marketing, and ads all tell the same story.
- **Persona discipline:** Forces clear separation between technical and business buyer messaging from the start, rather than retrofitting it later.
- **Reusable framework:** The same structure works for any product launch — just swap the input brief.

## How to Use It (Step by Step)

1. Open `input.txt` and fill in your product details:
   - Product or feature name
   - What it does (2-3 sentences)
   - Who it is for
   - Key differentiator vs competitors
   - Launch date (if known)

2. Run the following prompt:
   > "Read input.txt and generate a full GTM launch kit using the instructions in gtm-generator.md. Save the output to gtm-output.txt"

3. Review `gtm-output.txt` — it will contain all 7 sections, clearly labelled and ready to adapt.

## The 7 Output Sections

1. **Positioning Statement** — A single paragraph answering: who it's for, what it does, and why it's different.
2. **Messaging Pillars** — Three core themes, each with a name, description, and proof point.
3. **Persona Messaging** — Separate headlines, supporting points, and CTAs for technical and business buyers.
4. **Launch Email to Existing Customers** — Subject line, preview text, body copy, and CTA, ready to send.
5. **Internal Sales Enablement One-Pager** — Product overview, pain points, benefits, competitive differentiators, and objection handling for the sales team.
6. **Ad Headline Variations** — Five headline options split across pain-point and benefit angles, for both personas.
7. **Suggested Launch Timeline** — A week-by-week plan from internal prep through post-launch follow-up.

## Persona-Aware by Design

This tool is built around the idea that technical buyers and business buyers need different messaging — not just different tone, but different priorities and proof points. Every relevant section (persona messaging, ad headlines, sales enablement) clearly separates **Technical Buyer** content from **Business Buyer** content, so nothing gets mixed or generic.

# GTM Launch Kit Generator

## How to use
1. Paste your product or feature description into input.txt
2. Run this prompt: "Read input.txt and generate a full GTM 
   launch kit using the instructions in gtm-generator.md. 
   Save the output to gtm-output.txt"

## What to include in input.txt
- Product or feature name
- What it does in 2-3 sentences
- Who it is for
- Key differentiator vs competitors
- Launch date if known

## GTM Launch Kit Instructions

Read input.txt and produce all of the following:

### 1. Positioning Statement
One paragraph. Answer these three questions:
- For who?
- What does it do?
- Why is it different?

### 2. Messaging Pillars (3 pillars)
Each pillar needs:
- Pillar name (3 words max)
- One sentence description
- One proof point or example

### 3. Persona Messaging
Write separate messaging for each persona:

Technical Buyer:
- Headline (under 8 words)
- Two supporting points
- One call to action

Business Buyer:
- Headline (under 8 words)
- Two supporting points
- One call to action

### 4. Launch Email to Existing Customers
- Subject line (under 50 characters)
- Preview text (under 90 characters)
- Email body (150 words max)
- Clear CTA at the end

### 5. Internal Sales Enablement One-Pager
- Product overview (2 sentences)
- Top 3 customer pain points it solves
- Top 3 benefits with proof points
- Competitive differentiators (vs Adyen, Braintree, PayPal)
- 3 objection handling responses
- Suggested demo talking points

### 6. Ad Headline Variations (5 headlines)
- Under 8 words each
- Mix of pain-point led and benefit led
- One version for technical buyer
- One version for business buyer

### 7. Suggested Launch Timeline
- Week minus 2: internal prep tasks
- Week minus 1: pre-launch tasks
- Launch week: go-live tasks
- Week plus 1: post-launch tasks

## Output Format
- Save everything to gtm-output.txt
- Use clear headers for each section
- Add a divider between each section
- Label persona-specific content clearly

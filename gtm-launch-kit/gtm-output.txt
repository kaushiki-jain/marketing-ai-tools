1. POSITIONING STATEMENT
==================================================

For Irish SaaS companies expanding into EU and US markets, Stripe Tax automatically calculates, collects, and reports sales tax and VAT across every jurisdiction you sell into. Unlike manual processes or standalone tax tools, Stripe Tax runs natively inside your existing Stripe integration — no code changes, no third-party reconciliation, and tax rules that update automatically as laws change.

5. INTERNAL SALES ENABLEMENT ONE-PAGER
==================================================

Product Overview:
Stripe Tax automatically calculates, collects, and reports VAT and sales tax across multiple jurisdictions, working natively within existing Stripe integrations. It removes the need for manual tax management or standalone tax software as companies scale internationally.

Top 3 Customer Pain Points Solved:
1. Manually tracking VAT and sales tax rates across multiple countries.
2. Managing tax registration thresholds as the business grows into new markets.
3. Reconciling tax data between payment systems and separate tax tools.

Top 3 Benefits with Proof Points:
1. No code changes — activates within existing Stripe integration.
2. Automatic rate updates — stays current as tax laws change in real time.
3. Unified reporting — tax data sits alongside payment data for easy filing.

Competitive Differentiators:
- vs Adyen: Native to the payment flow rather than a bolt-on compliance layer.
- vs Braintree: No need for a separate third-party tax integration or reconciliation step.
- vs PayPal: Provides multi-jurisdiction registration tracking and filing-ready reports, not just basic tax collection.

Objection Handling:

Objection: "We already use a separate tax tool."
Response: Stripe Tax removes the reconciliation step between your payment and tax systems entirely, since both run on the same platform.

Objection: "We're not sure if we need to register in new jurisdictions yet."
Response: Stripe Tax monitors your transaction volumes and flags where registration thresholds are approaching, so you're never caught off guard.

Objection: "Switching tax systems sounds like a big project."
Response: Stripe Tax requires no code changes — it activates within your current Stripe integration, so there's no migration project.

Suggested Demo Talking Points:
- Show tax calculation happening automatically at checkout, no extra setup.
- Walk through the registration tracking dashboard for EU and US thresholds.
- Demonstrate exporting a filing-ready tax report.

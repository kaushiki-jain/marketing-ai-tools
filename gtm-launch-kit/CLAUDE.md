# GTM Launch Kit — Stripe Ireland

## Brand
- Company: Stripe
- Focus: Stripe's payment infrastructure for Irish SaaS and tech companies
- Industry: Fintech / Developer Tools / B2B SaaS

## Target Audiences
### Technical Buyer (Developer / CTO)
- Cares about: API quality, documentation, integration speed
- Language: Direct, technical, no fluff
- Pain points: Complex legacy payment systems, poor developer experience, unreliable uptime

### Business Buyer (Founder / CFO / Head of Growth)
- Cares about: Revenue optimisation, conversion rates, global expansion
- Language: Outcome-focused, ROI-driven
- Pain points: Failed payments, high transaction fees, limited market reach

## Stripe Brand Voice
- Intelligent and direct — no hype, no buzzwords
- Respects the reader's intelligence
- Uses plain language for complex ideas
- Never use: "revolutionary", "game-changing", "cutting-edge", "seamlessly"

## Irish Market Context
- Strong SaaS and fintech ecosystem in Dublin
- EU market access is a key selling point
- Competitors: Adyen, Braintree, PayPal, local bank payment solutions

## Output Rules
- Always produce clearly labelled sections
- Technical buyer content separate from business buyer content
- Keep copy concise — these are busy professionals
- Save all outputs as clearly named files

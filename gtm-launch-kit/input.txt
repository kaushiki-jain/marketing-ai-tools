Product: Stripe Tax
What it does: Stripe Tax automatically calculates and collects
the correct amount of tax on transactions, handles tax
registration across multiple countries, and generates reports
for filing. It works directly within existing Stripe
integrations with no code changes required.
Who it is for: Irish SaaS companies scaling into EU and US
markets who are struggling with VAT and sales tax compliance
across multiple jurisdictions.
Key differentiator: Unlike manual tax calculation or standalone
tax tools, Stripe Tax is built directly into the payment flow —
no third party integrations, no reconciliation headaches,
real-time compliance updates as tax laws change.
Launch date: Q3 2026 campaign targeting Dublin SaaS companies
